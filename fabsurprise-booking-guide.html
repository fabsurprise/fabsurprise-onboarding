A Few Important Details

Every celebration is different, but here's what's helpful to know before we begin.

Your booking is confirmed once we receive your payment. Until then, your date remains available to other enquiries, since planning only begins once your booking is confirmed.

From that moment, we begin planning your celebration. Depending on your booking, we may start ordering items, booking vendors, preparing personalised gifts, and coordinating everything needed to bring it to life.

Full payment is our standard option for most bookings.

For some larger celebrations or certain corporate projects, we may agree on a payment plan before work begins. If that's the case for you, 80% confirms your booking and lets us start planning, and the rest is due before delivery, setup, or installation. This is the exception rather than the default, and we'll always talk it through with you directly first.

We love seeing your inspiration.

If you have reference photos or Pinterest ideas, feel free to share them with us. They'll help us understand the style you're looking for.

Rather than creating exact copies, we'll use them as inspiration to design something that feels uniquely yours, while considering your budget, available materials, and the overall vision for your celebration.

Need to make a change? We'll always do our best to help.

If the change affects the cost, timeline, or items we've already ordered, we'll explain everything clearly before making any changes.

If your celebration date changes, let us know as soon as you can.

We'll do our best to move your booking to another available date. If extra costs are involved, we'll let you know before moving forward.

Plans change, and we understand. If you need to cancel, please let us know as soon as possible.

We'll take a look at what's already been arranged on our end, like work completed, items ordered, or vendors booked, before we talk through the next steps together.

Every celebration is different, so refunds are considered individually rather than by a fixed formula.

If a refund is requested, here's what shapes the outcome:

  • Work already completed
  • Items already purchased
  • Personalised or custom-made pieces
  • Vendor and delivery costs already committed

If a refund is available, we'll walk you through exactly how it was calculated.

We carefully plan every delivery and setup to arrive as close to the agreed time as possible.

Occasionally, factors such as traffic, weather, venue access, or other unexpected circumstances may affect timings.

If this happens, we'll keep you updated every step of the way.

If your booking includes decor or setup, please make sure the venue is accessible at the agreed time.

Delays caused by restricted access, unavailable parking, late entry, or venue limitations may affect setup times, and could result in additional waiting charges where applicable.

Many of the items we use are handcrafted, fresh, seasonal, or customised.

Because of this, small variations in flowers, balloons, colours, packaging, or decorative details may occasionally occur. When they do, we'll always maintain the overall style and quality of your celebration.

Although every celebration is carefully planned, there are times when events beyond anyone's control can affect plans. This could include severe weather, road closures, government restrictions, supplier emergencies, or other unforeseen situations.

If anything like this happens, we'll communicate with you as quickly as possible and work with you to find the best way forward.

Some celebrations are deeply personal, and we completely understand the importance of privacy.

During your celebration, we may capture photos or videos to document and showcase our work.

If you'd like your celebration to remain private, simply let us know before your celebration. We'll respect your preference.

We may still share behind the scenes moments, such as the decor, styling, gifts, or setup, without revealing you, your loved ones, or any confidential information.

If you have specific privacy requirements, please let us know before your celebration so we can plan accordingly. If you contact us after your celebration with a concern about content we've shared, we'll review your request carefully and do our best to resolve it in a way that's fair and respectful to everyone involved.

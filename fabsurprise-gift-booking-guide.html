A Few Important Details

Every gift is different, but here's what's helpful to know before we begin.

Your order is confirmed once we receive your payment. Until then, your delivery date remains available to other enquiries, since preparation only begins once your order is confirmed.

From that moment, we begin preparing your gift. Depending on your order, this may include sourcing items, arranging personalisation, and coordinating everything needed for delivery.

Full payment is required to confirm your gift delivery order.

For larger or corporate gift orders, we may occasionally agree on a payment plan before work begins. If that's the case for you, we'll always talk it through with you directly first.

Need to make a change? We'll always do our best to help.

If the change affects the cost, timeline, or items we've already ordered, we'll explain everything clearly before making any changes.

If your preferred delivery date changes, let us know as soon as you can.

We'll do our best to move your delivery to another available date. If extra costs are involved, we'll let you know before moving forward.

Plans change, and we understand. If you need to cancel, please let us know as soon as possible.

We'll take a look at what's already been arranged on our end, like work completed, items ordered, or vendors booked, before we talk through the next steps together.

Every order is different, so refunds are considered individually rather than by a fixed formula.

If a refund is requested, here's what shapes the outcome:

  • Work already completed
  • Items already purchased
  • Personalised or custom-made pieces
  • Vendor and delivery costs already committed

If a refund is available, we'll walk you through exactly how it was calculated.

We carefully plan every delivery to arrive as close to the agreed time as possible.

Occasionally, factors such as traffic, weather, or other unexpected circumstances may affect timings.

If this happens, we'll keep you updated every step of the way.

Many of the items we use are handcrafted, fresh, seasonal, or customised.

Because of this, small variations in flowers, colours, packaging, or decorative details may occasionally occur. When they do, we'll always maintain the overall style and quality of your gift.

Although every order is carefully planned, there are times when events beyond anyone's control can affect plans. This could include severe weather, road closures, government restrictions, supplier emergencies, or other unforeseen situations.

If anything like this happens, we'll communicate with you as quickly as possible and work with you to find the best way forward.

Some gifts are deeply personal, and we completely understand the importance of privacy.

While preparing your gift, we may capture photos or videos to document and showcase our work.

If you'd like your gift and delivery to remain private, simply let us know when you place your order. We'll respect your preference.

We may still share behind the scenes moments, such as the gift itself, packaging, or preparation, without revealing you, your recipient, or any confidential information.

If you have specific privacy requirements, please let us know when you place your order so we can plan accordingly. If you contact us afterward with a concern about content we've shared, we'll review your request carefully and do our best to resolve it in a way that's fair and respectful to everyone involved.

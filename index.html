Our View

There is no one way to celebrate.

What makes a moment special is not how much you spend or how elaborate it is. It is how well it reflects the person, the relationship, or the reason behind it.

That is why we don't start with a package. We start with what matters.

The Fabsurprise Method
01

Start with the reason.

You tell us what you're thinking about, who it's for, what you want to celebrate and anything you already have in mind. You don't need to have the whole idea figured out.

02

We get to know the moment.

We ask the right questions so we understand the person, the relationship, the occasion or reason, the setting and what you want the moment to say.

03

We create the idea.

We take what we've learned and develop the right concept for the moment. Nothing is pulled from a standard catalogue simply because it's available.

04

We handle the details.

Once the direction is agreed, we bring the pieces together, coordinate the people involved and manage the details required to make it happen.

05

You experience the moment.

You don't have to carry the logistics. You get to be present for the part that matters and see the idea become real.
